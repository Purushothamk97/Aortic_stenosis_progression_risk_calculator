Aortic Stenosis Progression Risk Calculator

This calculator estimates the annual decline in Aortic Valve Area (AVA) based on collated research data points from studies on AS progression. Weights are derived from regression coefficients where available (e.g., age, CKD, AF) and approximated for modifiable factors like diabetes and hypertension based on hazard ratios and associations from meta-analyses. A more negative decline indicates faster progression. Risk categories: High (<-0.12 cm²/year, likely rapid progression), Medium (-0.08 to -0.12), Low (>-0.08, slower than average).

References (Ordered by Importance in Model)

  1. Venema CS, et al. Prediction of the Individual Aortic Stenosis Progression Rate and its Association With Clinical Outcomes. JACC: Advances. 2024. (Also available on PubMed Central. Core multivariable regression model for predictors like age, CKD, AF; median AVA decline and risk categories.)
    Key Evidence

    Median AVA decrease: 0.09 cm²/y (IQR: 0.04-0.15 cm²/y); rapid progressors: 0.15 cm²/y vs. slow: 0.04 cm²/y. Multivariable model: Older age (-0.013 cm²/y per 10 years), AF (-0.021 cm²/y), CKD (-0.059 cm²/y), higher LVMI (-0.019 cm²/y per 50 g/m²), higher SVI (-0.008 cm²/y per 10 mL/m²). Rapid progression linked to higher mortality (HR 1.77) and AVR (HR 3.44). Predictors ranked by strength: CKD (-0.059), AF (-0.021), Age per 10y (-0.013), LVMI per 50 g/m² (-0.019), SVI per 10 mL/m² (-0.008).

  2. Capoulade R, et al. Aortic Stenosis Progression: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. JACC: Cardiovascular Imaging. 2023.
    Key Evidence

    Pooled AVA decline: -0.08 cm²/year (95% CI: 0.06-0.10). Faster in severe baseline AS (0.09 cm²/year) vs. mild (0.07 cm²/year).

  3. Yan AT, et al. Association Between Cardiovascular Risk Factors and Aortic Stenosis: The CANHEART Aortic Stenosis Study. JACC. 2017.
    Key Evidence

    Hypertension: aHR 1.71, PAR 23.4%. Diabetes: aHR 1.49, PAR 5.6%. Dyslipidemia: aHR 1.17, PAR 4.4%. Combined PAR 34.4%.

  4. Ko T-Y, et al. Incidence, risk factors and predictors of cardiovascular mortality for aortic stenosis among patients with diabetes mellitus. Diabetes Research and Clinical Practice. 2022.
    Key Evidence

    In T2DM patients, AS incidence: 3.02 cases/1000 person-years. Risk factors include AF (aHR 1.69), statin protective (aHR 0.78).
